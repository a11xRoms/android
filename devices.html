Supported Devices

Initial support is focused on a small set of Galaxy devices to keep development tight and performance‑oriented. More devices may be added later once the base experience is stable.

Galaxy S10e
Exynos / Snapdragon variants
Compact flagship with strong performance and great ROM potential. Ideal testbed for lightweight, fast builds.
Galaxy S10+
Exynos / Snapdragon variants
Larger battery and display, perfect for users who want a premium feel with a tuned ROM experience.
Galaxy Note 20
Exynos / Snapdragon variants
Productivity‑focused device with S‑Pen support. BeyondROM aims to keep the Note experience intact while trimming unnecessary bloat.
Galaxy S20
Exynos / Snapdragon variants
Modern flagship with strong hardware and camera. A great candidate for performance‑tuned ROM builds.
Device list is subject to change as development evolves.
