About BeyondROM

BeyondROM is a personal ROM project focused on Samsung Galaxy devices, starting with the S10 series and expanding to newer flagships like the Note 20 and S20.

The goal is simple: deliver a clean, fast, and stable experience with modern animations and thoughtful debloat, without turning the device into a broken experiment.

Design philosophy

• Keep the UI familiar but smoother.
• Remove bloat while preserving essential features.
• Focus on performance, battery, and responsiveness.
• Take inspiration from HyperOS, One UI, and AOSP.
